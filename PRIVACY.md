# Privacy Policy — Tirumala SevaPilot

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated**: September 23, 2026

Tirumala SevaPilot ("the Extension") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of its users. This policy outlines how information is handled.

---

## 1. Zero External Data Collection
Tirumala SevaPilot does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or monitor any personal user data. We do not operate remote tracking servers, third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics), or error-reporting services that receive user information.

---

## 2. Information Handled Locally
When you choose to configure pilgrim profiles, the following categories of information may be stored on your local device:
- Pilgrim Name, Gender, Date of Birth / Age
- Government Identity Document type and number (such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport)
- Contact information (Mobile number, optional Email address)
- Address details (City, State, PIN code)

All of the above information is stored **strictly inside your browser's local sandbox** via `chrome.storage.local`.

---

## 3. How We Use Permissions
- `storage`: Enables persistent local saving of your profiles, group preferences, and settings.
- `sidePanel`: Provides the devotional side panel interface for convenient multi-tasking alongside the booking window.
- `activeTab` & `scripting`: Allows scanning and filling of form inputs only when you are actively browsing official TTD booking pages.
- Host permissions (`ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in`): Limits the extension's execution exclusively to authentic TTD domains.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion
You maintain complete control over your data:
- You can edit or delete individual pilgrims or profiles at any time.
- You can clear all data instantly via the **Clear All Data** option in the Extension Settings.
- Uninstalling the extension immediately wipes all local storage data from your device.

---

## 5. Contact
For questions regarding this privacy policy or open-source verification, please visit our project repository.
